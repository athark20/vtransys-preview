Fuel testing solutions · designed by VTransys

Verify every blended litre.

Ethanol is a cleaner-burning fuel that lowers carbon emissions. India's blending programme set out to roughly double ethanol production, backed by about USD 7 billion of planned investment, and E20 is now the norm at the pump.

That shift puts blend verification on the critical path for refiners, distilleries, OEM fuel labs and quality-control teams. Our analysers measure ethanol, biodiesel and isobutanol content quickly, accurately and in the field.

Flagship

ECMS009

ECMS009 NEXXT ethanol content measuring system

Accurate ethanol measurement for laboratories and fuel testing facilities, including the ethanol concentration of blended commercial fuel.

  • Fast ethanol detection
  • Installs in about 5 minutes
  • Powered by 12 to 18 V DC or 230 V AC
  • Simultaneous fuel temperature measurement

Applications: fuel testing labs, distilleries, automotive fuel research, quality control

Biodiesel

FAME %

Biodiesel % monitoring system

High-precision measurement of the biodiesel (FAME) blend ratio in petroleum diesel, for fuel testing labs and engine test cells. Uses near-infrared (NIR) optical spectroscopy or ultrasonic velocity measurement rather than simple capacitance sensing.

  • High blend detection accuracy
  • Helps prevent fuel filter gelling in cold conditions
  • Improves engine calibration and emissions data
