Questions engineers ask

Frequently asked questions

What is isobutanol blending?

Isobutanol is an advanced biofuel that can be blended into diesel. Monitoring the blend ratio keeps engine calibration and emissions data valid during testing.

Who is this system for?

Fuel testing laboratories and engine test cells that evaluate advanced biofuel blends.

Can the same lab run ethanol and biodiesel measurements?

Yes. VTransys also supplies the ECMS009 NEXXT for ethanol in gasoline and the Biodiesel % monitoring system for FAME in diesel.

Is support available in India?

Yes. Installation, calibration services and 24/7 technical support are provided from Pune.
