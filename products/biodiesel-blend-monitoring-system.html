Biodiesel (FAME) percentage monitoring system for diesel blends

A high-precision system for measuring the biodiesel (FAME) blend ratio in petroleum diesel, built for fuel testing laboratories and engine test cells. Because diesel and biodiesel have similar electrical properties, it uses near-infrared (NIR) optical spectroscopy or ultrasonic velocity measurement rather than simple capacitance sensing.

Questions engineers ask

Frequently asked questions

What blend ratios can it monitor?

It measures the percentage of biodiesel (FAME) in petroleum diesel. Request the datasheet for the measurement range of the configuration that fits your fuels.

Why NIR or ultrasonic instead of a capacitance sensor?

Diesel and biodiesel have very similar electrical properties, so a capacitance sensor cannot distinguish them reliably. NIR spectroscopy and ultrasonic velocity respond to the chemical and physical differences instead.

How does it help with filter gelling?

Knowing the true FAME percentage lets operators manage blends in cold conditions, where high biodiesel content raises the risk of fuel filter gelling.

Where is it typically installed?

In fuel testing laboratories and engine test cells, where blend ratio feeds directly into calibration and emissions results.

Does VTransys install and support it?
