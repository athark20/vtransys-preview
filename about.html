Empowering Indian manufacturing with global engineering precision.

VTRANSYS Solutions is a technology sales and services company serving the automobile and manufacturing sectors. We bridge the gap between what engineering teams need to measure and the advanced technology providers who build the instruments to do it.

Company profile

A small team with 23 years in automotive R&D sales.

VTransys was founded to put the team's accumulated skills to work for Indian industry and to represent advanced technology firms that were under-served here. Our focus is on understanding each client's strategy first, then matching it with the right measurement infrastructure.

That approach has built a strong industrial customer base across automotive OEMs, Tier 1 suppliers, research institutes and fuel testing laboratories, with most of our growth coming from repeat business and referrals.

Our journey

From a single product line to three international partnerships in four years.

  1. 2021

    VTransys established

    Founded in Pune to bring high-end precision measurement technology to India's automotive and manufacturing sectors.

  2. 2023

    ONO SOKKI, Japan

    Appointed authorised Indian distributor for sound, vibration, RPM and torque measurement instruments.

  3. 2024

    ALMA Automotive, Italy

    Added combustion analysis, powertrain test and HIL systems for engine and EV development programmes.

  4. 2025

    TRIGAS DM, Germany

    Added turbine flowmeters, calibrators and flow computers for liquid and gas measurement.
